Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Effective Date / 生效日期：2026-11-22 · Applies to Quotlet for iOS, Android, macOS, and app.quotlet.com

Summary: Quotlet only stores the minimum account, quota, and session metadata required to sync your AI coding tool usage between your Mac and mobile devices. We do not sell your data, run third-party advertising SDKs, or track you across apps.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account Information: Your verified email address (via one-time verification code) and/or your GitHub account ID, display name, and verified email when you sign in with GitHub.
  • Device Metadata: Device name, platform (Mac, iOS, Android, Web), app version, a hashed device identifier (to avoid duplicate device entries), and remote command capability flags.
  • Usage & Session Metadata: AI quota percentages and reset timestamps, daily token counts by project name (directory name only, not full filesystem paths) and model, session status timelines, and remote command prompts/outputs that you explicitly initiate.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We never upload your Claude, Codex, or Gemini API keys, OAuth tokens, or source code repositories to Quotlet Cloud.
  • We do not collect advertising identifiers (IDFA / AAID), precise location, contacts, photos, or behavioral analytics for ad targeting.

3. How Data Is Stored & Shared

  • Data is transmitted over HTTPS (TLS) and stored in Cloudflare D1 solely to provide synchronization and remote command delivery across your signed-in devices.
  • Authentication tokens are stored locally in your device Keychain (iOS/macOS) or encrypted via Android Keystore (Android).
  • Transactional login verification emails are delivered via Resend; GitHub OAuth is used only if you choose "Sign in with GitHub".

4. Retention & Account Deletion

  • Session status timelines are retained for 30 days; quota history, sessions, and remote commands for 90 days; daily token summaries for 100 days.
  • You can revoke any device at any time from the Devices tab.
  • You can permanently delete your entire account and all associated cloud data directly inside the iOS or Android app under Devices → Delete Account (or via DELETE /api/me).

5. Contact

For privacy inquiries or data deletion assistance, please contact us via support@quotlet.com or open an issue at github.com/tluo-github/quotlet.
